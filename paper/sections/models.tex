\section{Models}\label{sec:models}

\subsection{Response-function superposition}\label{sec:response}
% Source: src/models/response.py (docstring); docs/analysis_plan.md §14 items 18-20.

The main model is a hybrid: a sum of responses to stresses, as in transfer-function noise models
\citep{Collenteur2019Pastas}, written as one differentiable model and fitted for every well at once.
The head of well $i$ minus its training mean is
\begin{equation}
h_i(t) - \mu_i = d_i + \sum_{r} G_{ir}\,\bigl(k_{ir} * s_{ir}\bigr)(t),
\label{eq:response}
\end{equation}
where $r$ runs over the stresses (recharge, pumping, river, canal), $s_{ir}$ is the input that
reaches well $i$ for stress $r$, $k_{ir}$ a gamma kernel on the weekly grid with non-negative
weights that sum to one, and $G_{ir}$ the gain, the head change under a sustained unit stress. The
recharge input is the well's effective rain $P - f_i\,\mathrm{ETP}$ with an evaporation factor
$f_i$. Gains and kernels cannot trade scale, the kernels' time scales are bounded more widely than
any published response of this aquifer \citep{Caballero2015Impact}, and each contribution
$G_{ir}(k_{ir} * s_{ir})$ is exact and additive, so the model's own decomposition of a head into its
drivers is available at every week. That decomposition explains the model's computation; it is
not, by itself, the physical contribution of a driver, which is what the twin tests
(\cref{sec:twin}). The loss is the mean over wells of the training MSE divided by each well's
training variance, so that wells with large and small swings weigh alike.

\subsection{A ladder of knowledge}\label{sec:ladder}

The hybrid is built in rungs, each adding one kind of knowledge to the same model, so that a gain
can be traced to what was added:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Free}: one set of parameters per well, inputs from the well's own weather cell and the
  pumping within \SI{3}{km}, signs by construction.
\item \emph{Pooled}: the parameters are pulled towards the mean of the well's aquifer group with a
  penalty $\lambda$; one response per group is the other end of the scale.
\item \emph{Facts}: the parameters of a well are its group mean plus a function of its declared
  facts plus a small deviation. Fact set A comes from the dataset (depth, altitude, position in the
  stack); set B adds the facts the knowledge graph holds. The comparison of B with A is the
  ``knowledge as information'' arm of RQ1. Two controls guard it: random facts of the same width
  (name tags) and the facts of other wells, both drawn per start \citep{Heudorfer2024On}; and a
  well left out of training keeps a zero deviation, so its responses come from its group and its
  facts alone.
\item \emph{Routing}: the inputs of each well are routed by the edges of a graph arm
  (\cref{sec:kg-export}), arm~C's distance rules or arm~D's knowledge-graph edges, on otherwise the
  same model. This is the primary form of the D versus C contrast: two runs that differ only in the
  arm differ only in which sources reach each well. Arms E and F are controls built from D's edges:
  E rewires them, either preserving every degree or matching each edge's length, so that it keeps
  the structure or the locality of D and loses the meaning; F draws random edges or links every
  compatible pair.
\item \emph{Message passing}: the facts of a well's linked neighbours, averaged over the arm's
  well links \citep{Hamilton2018Inductive}, also feed the function of rung 3 (secondary).
\end{enumerate}
The ontology-derived graph thus reaches the model in two ways, as information (rung 3) and as
structure (rungs 4 and 5), as the research questions require. % Gate 3 point 2 confirms rungs 3-5
% and the routing contrast as primary; point 20 decides the bounded residual network.

\subsection{Sign constraints}\label{sec:signs}

The ontology's mechanism specifications give the direction of each response: abstraction from a
connected unit does not raise the head, effective rain does not lower it, and where a source
documents them, river exchange and canal return flow do not lower it. In this study a sign may
constrain a model only where the knowledge graph marks the influence as eligible and the export
policy accepts it (\cref{sec:kg-rules}): at present every exact-unit pumping influence, and the
recharge, river and canal signs whose adoption is a decision of the review of the knowledge graph.
% Gate 3 points 7-10 (recharge, rivers, canals, proxy adequacy).
Three settings answer RQ2. \emph{Hard}: the signs hold by construction, the gain of a decreasing
response being $-\mathrm{softplus}(\cdot)$ and no parameter depending on a stress, so that the
monotonicity holds along the whole path from input to head \citep{Runje2023Constrained}; a
finite-difference test checks it. \emph{None}: every gain is a free number. \emph{Soft}: free
gains plus a penalty, $\lambda_s$ times the share of each well's training variance carried by
contributions of the wrong sign, a counterfactual form of the explanation penalties of
\citet{Ross2017Righta} in the loss's own units. % Gate 3 point 6: the grid of lambda_s on real data.

\subsection{Baselines}\label{sec:baselines}

The hybrid is compared with references a hydrologist and a machine-learning reader would each
expect: the seasonal naive forecast and the training climatology; Pastas per well
\citep{Collenteur2019Pastas}, with a reference built from the training heads only and the start of
each well chosen by a frozen rule; LightGBM \citep{Ke2017Lightgbm} without the well's own
climatology; a global LSTM \citep{Hochreiter1997Long,Wunsch2021Groundwater} and an entity-aware
LSTM \citep{Kratzert2019Towards}; Chronos-2 \citep{Ansari2025Chronos}, zero-shot, with
cross-learning and fine-tuned, the reference of the forecasting task; and two spatio-temporal graph
networks, one on a three-nearest-neighbour graph and one following \citet{Taccari2024Spatial}.
Every learned baseline had the same tuning budget, 24 Optuna trials \citep{Akiba2019Optuna} and a
re-selection over three seeds. Finally, the Taccari-style network run on arm~D's edges is the
black box on the same graph: it sees the same sources and links as the hybrid of arm~D, without
the response structure or the signs. % Gate 3 point 21: its scope (R1, 5 seeds, one Colab GPU session).
