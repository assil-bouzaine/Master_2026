Description

  • Country-agnostic explainability module of the LULC-Water network (since 0.12.0): model runs, simulated heads and their SGI classes, driver contributions, explanation statements with their status (CQ10), and explicit crop-change scenarios with their assumptions, eligibility and results (CQ16).

  • Module d'explicabilité indépendant des pays du réseau LULC-Water (depuis 0.12.0) : exécutions de modèles, niveaux simulés et leurs classes IPS, contributions des forçages, énoncés d'explication et leur statut (CQ10), et scénarios explicites de changement de culture avec leurs hypothèses, leur éligibilité et leurs résultats (CQ16).

  • Values of lulcw:AssumptionKind (CQ16).

  • Values of lulcw:ContributionDirection.

  • Mapping of every entry in src.models.hybrid_gnn.CHANNELS: recharge_local -> lulcw:LocalRechargeStress, recharge_regional -> lulcw:RegionalRechargeStress, recharge_leakage -> lulcw:LeakageRechargeStress (all skos:broader lulcw:RechargeStress, driver group lulcw:RechargeProxies); pumping -> lulcw:AttributedPumpingStress, pumping_unattributed -> lulcw:UnattributedPumpingStress (lulcw:SummerDemand); river_c and river -> lulcw:RiverStress (lulcw:RiverDischargeGroup); canal_c and canal -> lulcw:CanalStress (lulcw:SummerDemand). Optional bounded residual maps to lulcw:SeasonalResidual (no driver group).

  • Values of lulcw:ModelStress, covering both the aggregated v4 stresses and the v5 Hybrid-GNN v2 channels.

  • Values of lulcw:RankingMetric (CQ16).

  • Values of lulcw:ReferenceCondition.

  • Values of lulcw:SgiClass, ordered from very low (notation 1) to very high (notation 7).

  • Values of lulcw:StatementStatus.
