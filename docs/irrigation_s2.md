## 1. Rule and frozen threshold

For each parcel-year of the Sentinel-2 parcel tables (`2017–2025`, RPG parcels intersecting at least one well's 3 km buffer):

1. **Quality:** `insufficient_data` if `n_px_summer < 4` (under ~400 m² of valid 10 m pixels), `nobs_summer < 3` (fewer than 3 clear July–August acquisitions), or if `ndvi_summer` or `SWI_JA` is missing.
2. **Dry conditions:** each parcel is linked to its nearest SAFRAN cell centre in Lambert-93; `SWI_JA` is the mean weekly `SWI` over July and August of that year (`forcing_weekly.parquet`). `not_applicable` if `SWI_JA > 0.5`: above half the available soil water, a green summer canopy does not need irrigation (FAO-56 depletion fraction p ≈ 0.5; Allen et al. 1998, FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56).
3. **Positive / negative:** `positive` if `ndvi_summer >= TAU`, else `negative`, where **`TAU = 0.4850`** is Otsu's threshold on a 0.005-wide NDVI histogram over `[-0.2, 1.0]`, computed once on the quality-passing, dry parcel-years of the **training editions 2017–2019 only** (parcels unweighted) and frozen before any comparison with the census or 2020+ years.
4. **Sensitivity variants (reported only, never used for the static well fact):** `TAU - 0.05` (`0.4350`), `TAU + 0.05` (`0.5350`), and `TAU` with `ndmi_summer >= 0.0`.

### Caveats (CQ15)

1. **Partial typology coverage:** the RA2020 irrigating-farm typology covers **73 %** of dept 66's irrigated SAU, so true crop-level irrigated shares can exceed the upper bounds derived above.
2. **Spatial mismatch:** the Sentinel-2 parcel set covers the 65 wells' 3 km zones (concentrated on the Roussillon plain and valley floors), whereas the census covers the entire département.
3. **Unit mismatch:** the census reports irrigated area by **farm type** (specialised or mixed farms), not by individual parcel or crop.
4. **Summer bare soil on vegetable parcels:** market-gardening parcels (group 25) are often harvested in spring/early summer or tilled between crop rotations in July–August, so a low summer NDVI produces a `negative` status even on irrigated land (**a negative result does not prove rainfed management**).
5. **Cell-scale wetness and canopy confounding:** `SWI_JA` is an 8 km SAFRAN grid-cell mean, not field soil moisture; deep-rooted perennials, grass cover between vine rows, or mixed 10 m pixels can raise summer NDVI without irrigation.
6. **No water-source link:** a `positive` status never asserts groundwater pumping or canal supply (`irrigatedBy`).
