Description

  • Country-agnostic documentary and literature-derived causal-evidence vocabulary: source documents, verbatim passages, exchange statements, exchange directions, hedging, evidence methods, and unit mappings, ready to hold extracted Mediterranean causal axioms (R2.1).

  • Vocabulaire documentaire et des preuves causales issues de la littérature indépendant des pays : documents sources, passages cités, énoncés d'échange, sens d'échange, modalités, méthodes de preuve et correspondances d'entités, prêt à accueillir les axiomes causaux méditerranéens extraits (R2.1).

  • Values of lulcw:EvidenceMethod.

  • Values of lulcw:ExchangeDirection.

  • Values of lulcw:Hedging.

  • Values of lulcw:UnitMapping.

exchange statementénoncé d'échange c

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#ExchangeStatement
Description

A statement read from a document about water exchange between surface water (a river section, a watercourse or the canals) and hydrogeological units: its mechanism, direction, state or period, quantity, hedging, method, how the source names its subject, and the passage it comes from. The subject is a unit itself (lulcw:aboutUnit) or an aggregate of units (lulcw:aggregateMemberUnit, and lulcw:aboutGroundwaterBody when the source states a WFD body). The graph records what the source says, at the scale the source says it; it is not a measured effect, and statements that disagree are kept side by side.

aggregate member unitentité membre de l'agrégat op

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#aggregateMemberUnit
Description

A unit that belongs to the aggregate an exchange statement is about (a groundwater body of several units, or a group the source names, such as 'the Quaternary formations'). Membership does not say that this unit takes part in the exchange: the statement is localised to the unit only by a separate, stated inference (lulcw:localizedStatement), never by membership alone.
Domain exchange statement c
Range lulcw:HydrogeologicalUnit

applicable statementénoncé applicable op

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#applicableStatement
Description

An exchange statement about the association's unit itself (lulcw:aboutUnit) and about a section that holds reach pieces of the association's zone and watercourse (for a canal-unit association: a canal-return statement naming the unit). A statement measured at a site (lulcw:measuredAt) applies only to the associations of that site's zone. Statements about an aggregate are never applicable; see lulcw:localizedStatement.
Range exchange statement c

localised aggregate statementénoncé d'agrégat localisé op

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#localizedStatement
Description

Derived by a rule: a statement about an aggregate that the association's unit belongs to (lulcw:aggregateMemberUnit), localised to the unit because the association's river reaches (about a section that holds pieces of them in the zone) or canal reaches run on the unit's outcrop, where the unit is the member in contact with the surface water. A separate inference from lulcw:applicableStatement: it documents the exchange at the aggregate's scale and makes no response sign eligible until the localisation is reviewed (Gate 3).
Range exchange statement c
Defined In Module / Défini Dans Le Module LULC-Water Mediterranean literature causal-evidence module
