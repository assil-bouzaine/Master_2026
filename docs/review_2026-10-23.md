## 3. Research design

- **RQ1.** Keep the arms of `docs/analysis_plan.md` §10 (A–F). They separate knowledge as
  information (B vs A) from knowledge as structure (D vs C).
  - The refined pre-check shows that a knowledge-graph graph has something to add:
    - same unit within 5 km (QAP p = 0.048)
    - a depth difference (p = 0.002)
  - Arm D beats arm C only if the graph has edges below BD LISA level (depth) and for pumps
    (CQ2). Otherwise D ≈ C.
- **RQ2.** PiShield constrains outputs and cannot express the sign of a response to an input.
  - Make the hard arm monotone by construction.
  - The soft arm is the sign penalty of Ross et al. (2017), whose phrase the title borrows.
- **RQ3.** The concepts are the driver contributions, measured on a semi-synthetic twin. SGI
  accuracy is reported but cannot reveal a shortcut, because SGI is computed from the
  predicted head.
- **RQ4.** Pumping and the climatic deficit are collinear within a year. Explanations
  therefore name the driver group "summer demand" (`docs/analysis_plan.md` §8). The ontology
  must carry that group (§4.2, point 7).
- **Main model.** I recommend the hybrid of `docs/feedback.md` §5.5 as the main model, with
  the black-box HGT as a comparison. In the hybrid, the KG feeds a GNN that outputs the
  parameters of sign-constrained response functions, plus a small bounded residual. Why it
  is the best match for the ontology:
  - Every `lulcw:` causal relation does a job: it routes a stress and carries a sign.
  - The concepts are exact (RQ3) and the explanations exact (RQ4).
  - It answers the subject's "constraints" and "explanations" objectives directly.

  Cost: 2–3 weeks. This is a §3 decision, so it is Assil's and the supervisor's call.
- **Title.** Use "simulation", not "forecasting": Chronos-2 is the forecasting reference.

### 4.2 Modelling decisions to settle before writing Turtle

1. **Aquifer typing.**
   - BD LISA labels `671AA00` a "semi-permeable unit": its level-3 entity is missing and the
     level-2 name is used.
   - Yet it is the plain's main drinking-water aquifer. In GWML2 terms it is an **aquifer
     system** (sand aquifers plus clay confining beds).
   - So map BD LISA entities to GWML2 and SWEET types by a written rule (alluvium →
     unconfined aquifer, Pliocene → aquifer system, karst → aquifer, impermeable units →
     confining bed), not by `natureeh` alone.
2. **Depth is the investigation depth, not the screen depth.**
   - Hub'Eau's `profondeur_investigation` is the depth investigated by the borehole. The
     screened interval is only in the BSS logs.
   - The 10-20 review, `docs/kg_precheck.md` and today's draft all said "screen depth".
     Corrected in the CQs; the wording in the older documents should follow.
3. **Fixed depth classes are not supported by the data.**
   - In the pre-check, the class variable (0–60 / 60–150 / > 150 m) was not significant
     (QAP p = 0.52–0.54). The continuous depth difference was (p = 0.002).
   - So keep depth as a number. Derive "similar depth" edges from a |Δ depth| threshold
     chosen on the training period, or from Dem'Eaux layers if they are obtained.
4. **How causal relations and their sign are written.** Two levels:
   - **Class level:** "an abstraction decreases the head of the unit it pumps from".
   - **Instance level:** influence links, derived by SHACL-AF SPARQL rules with conditions
     (same unit, distance ≤ d, confidence).

   The links need attributes (sign, distance, confidence, evidence), and OWL property chains
   cannot test a distance. So use the W3C n-ary relation pattern: an `lulcw:Influence` node
   with its source, target, sign and evidence, rather than bare `decreasesHead` triples. The
   export turns each one into a typed edge with attributes.
5. **Provenance and confidence on every derived link** (`prov:wasDerivedFrom`,
   `lulcw:confidence`), checked by a SHACL shape. Main runs use confident links only.
6. **Time.** Land use (per RPG year), abstraction (per year) and restriction orders (dated)
   need explicit time. Use one pattern everywhere: SOSA observations with
   `sosa:phenomenonTime` (OWL-Time).
7. **Driver groups.** The ontology carries the group "summer demand" (deficit plus seasonal
   pumping). The templates must name the group, not its members, wherever the data cannot
   separate them (`docs/analysis_plan.md` §8).
8. **IRIs, metadata, licence.**
   - a persistent namespace (w3id.org if a redirect is approved, otherwise a GitHub Pages
     IRI)
   - `owl:versionIRI` and `dcterms` metadata
   - CC BY 4.0
   - French and English labels, and a `skos:definition` for every term
9. **Make "flawless" testable.** On every change, run in `tests/`:
   - a reasoner consistency check (HermiT or ELK, through ROBOT or owlready2)
   - an OOPS! pitfall scan
   - SHACL conformance
   - all CQ tests
   - the alignment P/R/F1
