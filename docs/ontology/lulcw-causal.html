Description

  • Country-agnostic causal-knowledge and model-constraint vocabulary: qualified driver-to-head influences, physical mechanisms, response signs, driver groups, constraint eligibility, export policies, constraint decisions, and observable driver properties.

  • Vocabulaire des connaissances causales et contraintes de modèle indépendant des pays : influences qualifiées, mécanismes physiques, signes de réponse, groupes de forçages, éligibilité aux contraintes, politiques d'export, décisions de contrainte et propriétés observables motrices.

  • Values of lulcw:ConstraintEligibility.

  • Values of lulcw:DriverGroup (docs/analysis_plan.md §8).

  • Values of lulcw:Mechanism: the four constraint families of the design (pumping drawdown, diffuse recharge, canal return flow, river exchange), and pathways named so that a proxy is never presented as the complete supply (lateral inflow, vertical leakage).

  • Values of lulcw:ResponseSign.

constraint decisiondécision de contrainte c

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#ConstraintDecision
Description

The decision of an export policy about the response signs of one mechanism: whether its driver was reviewed as an adequate stand-in for what the mechanism responds to (proxy review), and whether the eligible signs of the mechanism constrain the model (approval), each with its basis. A sign constrains a model only when its influence is selected by the policy, eligible by its pathway evidence (lulcw:constraintEligibility), and the decision for its mechanism accepts both the proxy and the constraint; a candidate (pending) decision constrains nothing.

influenceinfluence c

IRI https://w3id.org/lulcw#Influence
Description

A qualified driver-to-head pathway: a mechanism by which a named driver variable can change the head at a target, with its conditions, evidence path, response sign (possibly unknown) and constraint eligibility. A response sign refers to an increase of the driver with the stated conditions held fixed; it is not a measured causal effect.
